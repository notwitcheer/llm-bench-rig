2. A tall lighthouse on the island, built from voxels, with red and white stripes, a glass lamp room and a dark cap.
3. A light beam that sweeps a full circle every six seconds, with a soft glow around the lamp.

5. A small sailing boat with a sail that circles the island.
6. A sky that fades from deep blue to orange at the horizon, with twinkling stars, a moon, and two or three clouds drifting across.
7. Warm glowing windows on the lighthouse and a lit cottage beside it.
Keep it smooth (about 60 fps), with no console errors. Create index.html on your very first write and make a simple island render at once, then add the features one at a time so a working file always exists. Never split it into several files. You may check it with node --check or a quick script, but do not spend more than a few minutes verifying.
The working directory is __WORKDIR__. The final file must be exactly __WORKDIR__/index.html (copy this path, never retype it).
